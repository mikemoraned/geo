# The skew of the online pass detector

*Report of 2026-11-11, from the first evaluation runs that measure skew. It records how far the
passes the apps can see depart from ground truth, at two online pass radii.*

Skew is how far the passes the apps can see depart from ground truth. It describes the pass
detectors and the data, not the predictor. No predictor is chosen from it.

Ground truth matches each crossing within 250 m, and dates a pass by the visit's nearest sample.
The apps cannot read ground truth. Their online pass detector reports a crossing as passing while
the latest sample lies within its online pass radius of the crossing. A **visit** is a run of
consecutive samples that report one crossing. A pass is **seen** when a visit of its crossing
starts at or before the pass and ends at or after it. A visit is **unmatched** when it spans no
pass. The **offset** is the visit's start minus the pass's time.

Skew counts every pass, including those in a session's first minute, which scoring leaves out. Its
438,917 passes therefore exceed the 415,707 that the predictor is scored on.

## Findings

- **At 250 m, ground truth's own radius, the skew is close to none.** The online detector sees all
  but one pass. Its visit starts at the first sample within the radius, and ground truth dates the
  pass by the nearest. The offset's p10 and median are −10 s, one sample early, and its p90 is
  0 s.
- **At 100 m, the detector misses 23% of passes.** These are the passes whose nearest sample lies
  from 100 m to 250 m from the crossing. The passes it sees, it sees at the sample ground truth
  dates them by.
- **Few visits match no pass at either radius**: 183 at 100 m, and 40 at 250 m.
- **Measuring skew leaves the predictor's scores unchanged.** Both runs score the same as the
  apps' run `27013fff-1ae7-58c3-81ee-0098a4461cbd` on every measure and every count.

## Choice

The online pass radius is 250 m. It matches the radius within which ground truth matches a pass,
and at 250 m the online detector sees all but one pass.
