Announcing: Lookout

I travel a lot on trains, partly because of what you can see out the window. However, interesting things can often go by in seconds; one blink and you'll miss it. "Lookout" is designed to help with that.

The essence of the App is to be like a partner sitting opposite you who can see slightly further ahead.

An App like this is difficult to build because of the intermittent connectivity to the internet. So, Lookout is designed to rely on as little as possible: only GPS and an initial data download are required, and everything else is best-effort.

The current status of Lookout is that it's just about to enter V1 alpha1. So, think of this as a devblog on the road to V1.

I would normally prevaricate over saying anything about a project until it was "done done", but recent life events around me have made me re-appraise the preciousness of the time we have. I have done a lot already, so I thought I should be a bit more open in talking about things I haven't quite finished.

However, because of said life events, which are ongoing, expect these updates to be sporadic, as I fit this in with everything else!
